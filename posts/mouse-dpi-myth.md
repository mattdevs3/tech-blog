# Introduction 

For my project I will be researching and testing how different mouse DPI settings affect aiming and gaming performance. DPI is very advertised by mouse companies as a major selling point, supporting high DPIS. Many competitive and professional gamers use much lower dpi settings instead of high DPI like these companies advertise. This brings up a debated topic and question. Does using a higher DPI actually make and improve your aiming ability or is it just a marketing tactic. To come to a conclusion, I will personally test it myself by using a aim trainer called Kovaaks. I will be using the exact same mouse, and sensitivity using different DPI ranging from low to extremely high. I will also be using the same aim training scenarios so the test is consistent. My goal in this research project is to compare results and come to a conclusion on whether higher DPI has a noticeable impact on aiming performance. I will have multiple attempts at each DPI to account for other factors and get the most reliable testing as possible. Another part of my project that i want to explore is mouse polling rates. Many newer gaming mice are advertised as high polling rates such as 8000hz coming from the standard 1000hz. Higher polling rates give the ability to reduce input delay and the time between the mouse updates. This may sound good but it can also increase CPU usage and affect game performance depending on hardware. I also want to figure out why companies advertise these high DPI settings when none of their sponsored gaming athletes and many competitive players don't use them. Another conclusion I want to come to is whether increasing DPI reduces micro stuttering and is there a point to where continuously increasing DPI caps off and stops noticeable improvements.
This project will include my own testing along with research to figure out if higher DPI and polling rates have any competitive advantages and if whether they are worth it or not
